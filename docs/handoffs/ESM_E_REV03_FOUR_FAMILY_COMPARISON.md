## 1. Equal comparison profiles, not an engine ranking
GEMS is a language plus libraries; GP=GemsPy Linopy and GM=Antares Modeler standalone are runtime profiles. K=Antares Classic, H=hybrid Classic+GEMS, X=Xpansion annual LP-Benders and XT=Trajectory separately. PLEXOS phase profiles P-LT/P-PASA/P-MT/P-ST and P-SB26/P-CBA/IoSN (latter actual study configuration unavailable) distinguish software and application. PC=PyPSA Core+Linopy, E=Eur, DE=PyPSA-DE, O24=v0.9.2 2024 reference; published v0.9 outputs separate; O26=pinned 2026 development. TYNDP_Products M2/eGon/eDisGo/FlexPlan remain supplementary physical/data paths, not primary competing engines. Hybrid/federated is a hypothesis, not requirement.
