## 0. Entscheidungsvorschlag / Executive record

**E-D01 — Federated, not monolithic.** Kein einzelnes untersuchtes Produkt deckt Szenariotransformation, stochastischen Netzausbau, CBA, probabilistische Adequacy, AC-/N-1-/Stabilitätsprüfung und regionale Sektorkopplung mit nachgewiesener identischer Semantik vollständig ab. Die fachliche Einheit ist der Studienvertrag, nicht eine bestimmte Network-Datei oder ein einzelnes Python-Environment.

**E-D02 — Primärer allgemeiner Modellkern:** PyPSA/Linopy in einem modernen, gepinnten Releaseprofil; PyPSA-Eur/DE als bevorzugte sektorale Daten-/Modellbasis. Dies ist eine begründete Reuse-Empfehlung, keine benchmarkbasierte Überlegenheit. Open-TYNDP ist eine zyklusgebundene spezialisierte Referenzkette, nicht der neue universelle Fork. Eine Erweiterung über vorhandene Linopy-Constraints ist in der Regel einer neuen Engine vorzuziehen.

**E-D03 — Stochastik und Adequacy:** Antares Classic für probabilistische Markt-/Adequacy-Studien als eigenständige Referenzfamilie; Xpansion als bewährter Architekturansatz für LP-Benders und Trajectory-Master, unabhängig zu qualifizieren gegen eine kleine PyPSA-Extensive-Form. Keine Entscheidung über den Produktionssolver für große IoSN-Instanzen vor korrekten identischen Modellverträgen und Messdaten. ERAA/EVA ist ein eigener Prozess, nicht identisch mit two-stage investment oder einfacher ENS-Minimierung.

**E-D04 — Open-TYNDP-Reuse:** O24 (v0.9.2) gezielt für TYNDP-2024-NT-Referenz, bestehende Datenadapter, Projektfallbildung und B1–B4-Kandidaten; veröffentlichte v0.9-Ergebnisarchive separat. Offizielle 2026-CBA und IoSN bleiben HOLD: Referenz-Passthrough, Altjahresgewichte/fehlende Labelprüfung, nur erster Wetterfall im Standard-SB, 168h-Mittelung, fehlende Projekt-/Varianten-/Physikverträge. Kein Rückschluss aus erfolgreichem Optimize auf amtliche Methodikkonformität.

**E-D05 — Physische Netze:** TYNDP_Products-M2 nicht durch Open-TYNDP-Zonierung, NTC-Ketten oder allgemeines PyPSA-Clustering ersetzen. M2 hat eine dokumentierte, bereits unabhängig geprüfte synthetische MA1-Teilevidenz. Seine API-/I-Parameter-, MA2-, Realnetz-/Sicherheits- und Owner-Gates bleiben strikt bestehen. **E-D06 — GEMS gezielt statt Vollkonvertierung:** GEMS/GemsPy/Modeler als eigenständige deklarative LP/MILP-Modellfamilie, insbesondere für unabhängige kleine Matched-Model-Fälle und Antares-Integration. **E-D07 — Regionale Ebene:** eGon-data/ding0 für DE-Daten/synthetische Netze, eGo/eTraGo/eDisGo für bereits vorhandenen top-down und statisch bottom-up gekoppelten Referenzpfad. eDisGo bietet tatsächlich mehrperiodige radiale NC-/SOC-Branch-Flow-OPF, nicht nur Lastfluss; Slacks und Relaxationslücke sind zwingend zu prüfen. eTraGo-gekoppelte DSO-Links zuerst wiederverwenden. **E-D08 — Ein gemeinsames QA- und Datenlayer:** 50/50 Requirements explizit zugeordnet; 76 ursprüngliche Risiko-/Gap-Einträge auf 16 konsolidierte Arbeitsblöcke abgebildet; 97 vorhandene Benchmark-Tabellenzeilen in 20 Familien eingeordnet, plus 3 neue kleine analytische Falsifikatoren. ## 3. E3 — Mathematical reuse and reconciliation

### 3.1 Shared mathematical contract, not common internal code

For each asset a, location n, carrier c, period y, time t, scenario s, define ports q_{a,k,t,s} with explicit positive injection convention and units (MW, MW_th, MW_H2_LHV, kg/s, t/h as appropriate); conversion coefficients are dimensioned, not all efficiencies. Carrier balance Σ_{a,k∈(n,c)} q + exogenous supply − demand + ENS − spill = 0. Sign conversion occurs once at adapter boundary. An activity a with q_k=ν_k a (e.g. CHP, e-fuels, capture) shares the same dispatch/capacity variable across all ports. Independent links can violate this correlation.

Power/energy limits, existing/extendable status, service requirements, asset vintage, cost and information axes are explicit. For storage:

    e_t = (1−λ_t)^Δt e_prev + Δt(η_c p_c − p_d/η_d + inflow − spill)
    0 ≤ p_c ≤ P_c·a_c(t); 0 ≤ p_d ≤ P_d·a_d(t)
    E_min(t) ≤ e_t ≤ E_max(t); e_0/e_T or terminal value as specified.

Dispatch chronology Δt [h] is not statistical frequency ν_t or scenario probability π_s. If ν_t>1 for repeated representative periods, duration, transitions and carryover semantics must be explicitly constructed. Do not blindly use multiplicity in SOC. Different storage loss bases (daily/hourly) normalized. Cyclic closure is not interchangeable with physical initial/terminal states. StorageUnit fixed max_hours constrains E/P; Store+Links can separate P/E. Simultaneous charge/discharge excluded when service/physics demands it or relaxation qualified by proof/counterexamples.

Objective bookkeeping (fixed sunk vs variable cost, incremental vs total CAPEX, discount factor, annualisation, FOM, OPEX, starts, penalties, transfer payments, CO2 price, externality, salvage) defined separately from solver-specific objective constants. Weighted expectation with one master investment: min_X C_inv(X)+Σ_s π_s Σ_t ν_t Δt c_s,t(y_s); discount/investment period multipliers explicit. Do not put CAPEX into every scenario and count again in master. Cost attribution and reporting objectives distinct; compare canonical decomposition, not only scalar output. Under LP degeneracy validate feasible set/invariant costs and observables, not arbitrary identical primal/dual vectors.

UC adds u_t−u_prev=start_t−stop_t, min/max output conditional on status, min up/down and ramps with initial/elapsed status, reserve headroom/location/duration. PyPSA Core and Classic/GEMS have UC formulations; E/O active switches and exact cluster vs unit formulation differ. Ordinary LP-Benders operational subproblems do not support general integer recourse. A relaxed UC result cannot prove exact UC cost; select MILP extensive form or documented alternative with valid bounds, not force incompatible solver.

### 3.2 Stochastic investment — extensive form vs Benders vs trajectory

Common x chosen before weather realization; y_s conditional operation. Reference mathematical form:

    min Cᵀx + Σ_s π_s Q_s(x)
    Q_s(x)=min_y c_sᵀ y: A_s y_s ≥ b_s−B_s x, x∈X.
    Master cuts η_s ≥ Q_s(x̄)+g_sᵀ(x−x̄) valid for convex LP recourse with correct dual signs.
    For trajectory node n: x_n = x_parent + build_n − retire_n,
      lifetime/lead time and information-node equalities constrain allowable timing.

**Core:** PyPSA 2-stage capacity/dispatch and CVaR per C; use frontal extensive form as initial reference at small scale. No need to invent a new LP engine. **Xpansion:** weighted subproblem costs, master variables, reduced-cost subgradient and gap logic present (B §3.4); trajectory merger and scenario-tree costs present; its data contract is not PyPSA investment_periods. GemsPy Expectation unweighted in checked 0.4.0, its BendersRunner invokes Xpansion binary; it is not an independent pure-Python Benders solver. Master integer vs subproblem integer separate. Feasibility cuts/ENS slack policy and subproblem statuses recorded. For integer or nonconvex recourse, ordinary LP cuts not generally valid; choose compatible exact/relaxed variant, label lower bounds, or independently justify cuts. No a priori superiority of Benders for actual IoSN scale; compare solution/cost/residual, memory/time only after semantic equivalence, include deliberately unequal weights.

### 3.3 Network mathematics — transport, physical DC, flow-based and AC

- NTC: bounded directed transfer with declared efficiency, endpoint injections; no passive loop constraints. Classic areas/links, OET NTC, controllable PyPSA Links and GEMS transport compatible only for same signs/limits/constraints.
- Passive DC: f_l=(θ_i−θ_j−φ_l)/x_l on defined base, KCL; equivalently cycle-KVL Cᵀ(diag(x)f+φ)=0 (orientation consistent). PST is not arbitrary controllable HVDC transport. M2 affine x/c0 and fixed phase contract governed by its own decisions; c0 must have proper structural divergence-free/gauge meaning under that model, not fit arbitrary zonal transfers. Disconnected AC components each balance and fix gauge; HVDC endpoints are signed injections and not angle coupling. A_z Γ=P A_d (operator identity); disaggregation D with S·D=I on supported zonal injection subspace. Nonunique physical dispatch from a net position cannot be fabricated. M2 lossless-only synthetic input scope: reject lossy HVDC unless a separately approved extension, not erase loss; 220/380 vs 225/400 classification source attestation. Detailed network topology data absent from NTC.
- FBMC: H_z=H_nodal·GSK, CNEC-specific H_z NP + controlled-link terms ≤ RAM, net position balance per domain, baseline/reference flow offset and remedial action convention. DC line optimization not identical to zonal market coupling. Native PyPSA HEAD supports supplied PTDF/RAM/multiple domains (C §3). Checked v1.4.0 release lacks new file; minimal Linopy constraint adapter on release and separate development HEAD comparison are preferred to implementing another engine. GSK/CNEC/FRM/RAM/minRAM and cross-domain/reference logic still external, not CACM/JAO production certification. Classic binding constraints/GEMS can express linear domain rows, not automatically generate valid domains. M2/PTDF bridge from TYNDP_Products preserved as research path; check coverage, source topology and contingent islanding.
- AC and N-1: S_i=V_i conjugate(Σ Y_ij V_j), Q/voltage/taps/shunt/thermal MVA/reserve and losses. Core AC-PF can replay, not optimize unrestricted AC redispatch; DC SCLOPF BODF valid only for stated topology and nonislanding conditions. A feasible P schedule does not certify AC, contingency or dynamics. Use independent AC PF/OPF/redispatch with matched baseline, Δp±, losses and original states, preventive vs corrective actions. No slacks hidden as redispatch. TSO/DSO boundaries by actual asset IDs and consistent base. ### 3.4 Conversion vs independent model — reconcile B and C

B §5 gives direct counterexamples to a universal converter claim. It is not a criticism of GEMS as a language. Code supports Line/Transformer angle equations, despite obsolete docs excluding them. It does NOT support general PST phase terms (PyPSA v1.3.0 includes φ), multiport/process, link UC/ramp, arbitrary extra_functionality/Linopy model constraints, multi-period and unequal snapshot weights. B reported independent small LP reference calculations, not a target-engine run. E did not reproduce that CI or those reported solver runs.

### 7.3 Three additional analytic E falsifiers

**E:EX01 / F16 – under-voltage despite feasible DC transfer.** Base 1 MVA, slack V1=1 pu; single series branch r=.1 pu, x=.05 pu, tap1, no shunt, current/MVA rating 1 pu, illustrative V_min=.95 pu (not norm).
