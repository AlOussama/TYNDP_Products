## 1. Corrected comparative premise
Compare whole *qualified* ecosystems and deployed study profiles separately. GEMS is a declarative LP/MILP model language with reusable port equations, not a ready comprehensive sector library; Antares Classic is specialized mature electric dispatch/MC and Xpansion LP Benders; PLEXOS documented integrated asset/phases and TYNDP/ERAA application, licensed formulation not accessible; PyPSA Core is programmable algebraic component API, Eur/DE/OET and other research models constitute extensive independent reusable data/model/workflow portfolios. Specialized models cannot automatically be merged, and not every paper is executable. Distinguish L1 exogenous profiles, L2 balance/stock, L3 technology/time service states, L4 explicit physical network constraints at appropriate resolution, L5 detailed physical/dynamic models. These are descriptive depth labels, not scalar maturity scores. No production architecture selected.

Source basis: [PyPSA official model inventory](https://docs.pypsa.org/latest/home/models/), [GEMS libraries pinned c463e30b](https://github.com/AntaresSimulatorTeam/GEMS/tree/c463e30b610443c1f59b20074bb7acffa2844cce/libraries), [TYNDP26 scenario methodology §8](https://2026.entsos-tyndp-scenarios.eu/scenarios-methodology-report/modelling-methodologies/), [Eur transport/heating/hydrogen](https://pypsa-eur.readthedocs.io/en/latest/design/hydrogen/), [Eur heat](https://pypsa-eur.readthedocs.io/en/latest/design/heat/), [GEMS/Antares B](./ESM_B_20261127/ESM_B_ANTARES_GEMS_CAPABILITY_REVIEW.md), [Eur C](./ESM_C_PYPSA_OPEN_TYNDP_CAPABILITY_REVIEW.md), [DSO D](./ESM_D_DISTRIBUTION_GRID_SECTOR_COUPLING_REVIEW.md), [PLEXOS REV01](./ESM_E_REV01_PLEXOS_SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW.md). Andromede flex is pointwise curtailment cost, not energy-shifting service; electrolyser is constant efficiency q_H2=eta*p with upper p_max(t,s), no ramp, start or heat co-production. No distinct EV/HP/cold in four libraries inspected; custom models feasible but not supplied.

Reference EV: E_t=E_prev+dt*(eta_c p_c-p_d/eta_d)-E_drive; 0<=p_c<=a_t P_c, 0<=p_d<=a_t P_d, departure reserve and shared charger capacity. For individual EVs simultaneity may be prohibited; aggregate different vehicles may charge and discharge at once. Test disaggregation, availability during stress, no double-use for reserve/mobility, cost basis for sunk vehicle battery. TYNDP26 weekday commuter unavailable 09–16, non-Pkw profiles exogenous. National transition needs fleet adoption and charging infrastructure investment, adequacy needs deliverable flexibility (not nameplate SOC). 
Reference DSO radial BFM: P_ij=sum_child P_jk+p_j+r_ij l_ij, Q_ij=sum_child Q_jk+q_j+x_ij l_ij, v_j=v_i-2(rP+xQ)+(r^2+x^2)l, v_i l=P^2+Q^2 with v=|V|^2, l=|I|^2. SOC relaxation inequality requires gap/slack and AC replay. eDisGo OPF variants 1/3 do not establish hard V/I feasibility; 2/4 include bounds and slacks. Some Q tied to pf; no arbitrary independent P/Q or unbalanced/protection guarantee. Two feeder net-zero example: 2 MW load on 1 MW feeder and 2 MW PV elsewhere may overload despite zero regional import. Time-coupled P/Q flexibility envelopes need state/witness, topology and cost ownership. Do not equate aggregate LV links, wheeling, or DC grid with physical DSO.
Reference heat/cold: Qh=COP_t*p_h, Qc=EER_t*p_c; thermal stock and heat service balances; reversible equipment shares rating; building RC C(Tin_t-Tin_prev)/dt=(Tout-Tin_prev)/R+Qint+Qsolar+Qh-Qc with comfort bounds. COP/EER exogenous => LP; endogenous temperature-dependent efficiency may require valid piecewise or nonlinear model. No double-counting historic electric HVAC, shared CHP/PtH, cooling heat rejection Qrej=Qc+p_c and recoverable waste heat. Eur heat advantage does not imply cold implemented; USA closes model-structure gap partly. 6 MW heat at COP3 vs COP1.5 requires 2 vs 4 MW electricity; correlated temperature, RES and demand essential.
Reference H2: energy/mass balance and common LHV/HHV/units, electrolysis q=eta*p plus part load/ramp/min load where needed, import constraints, compressor and shared stoichiometric outputs, store and period boundaries; gas flow energy limit not pressure equation. Pipeline physical pressure relation and linepack (mass balance and pressure-linked storage), gas properties, compressor and retrofit disjoint usage separate. No proof of hydraulic H2 network in any main optimizer. For gas/H2 NEP use separate engineering validation. PLEXOS JRC example [JRC126071 (2022)](https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/bitstream/JRC126071/JRC126071_01.pdf), PLEXOS 8.1 DC/MILP electricity and steady-state gas mass flow, not current licensed H2 pressure proof (inherited/previously checked source; not rerun here).

## 5. Methodological impact on four-family assessment

| Dimension | GEMS | Antares | PLEXOS | Expanded PyPSA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Existing asset libraries | basic and legacy; transparent custom port equations | specialized hydrothermal cluster UC/stocks; hybrid GEMS | documented broad integrated catalogue; specific TYNDP/ERAA use, exact license/profile unknown | differentiated Eur/DE, USA, GB, IEI, local complements; strongest discovered open reuse breadth, not automatic compatibility |
| Market workflows | explicit custom LP/MILP, no complete market process | native ED/MC and X expansion | LT/PASA/MT/ST, actual scenario/adequacy uses | Core ED/UC, GB wholesale->balancing and capacity auction, stochUC DA/aFRR, NL2025 value stacking; no complete ERAA shown |
| Network | generic DC/linear ports; PST/converter guards, external physical solver | transport/binding, custom DC; external physical | D PTDF/LODF/N-1/SCUC, not full AC/DSO/H2 pressure proven | Core DC/KVL/PST/SCLOPF/FBMC HEAD; eGon/eDisGo explicit local; IEI H2 transport; no automatic AC or pressure solution |
| Stochastic | scenario indices, shared variables, GP/GM parity and weighting; X interface | native MC, X LP-Benders/trajectory | documented stochastic phases, ERAA U; exact joint x/recourse unknown | native common-x/CVaR, stochUC operational stages (old/incomplete modern), multiweather, separate MC workflow required |
| Robustness | custom CVaR/minimax mathematical expression | probabilistic outcomes, X decomposition; LP recourse | actual operational use, exact robust objective unknown | explicit finite scenario CVaR, historical multiweather near-optimal intersection, MGA alternatives; robust optimization != MC, MGA or N-1 |
| Numerical robustness | interpreter consistency and sparse/dense build tests | Benders bounds, hydro/UC mode, MC convergence | licensed LP/MPS exports and logs possible, access open | open equations/CI and custom constraints; multiple versions, test status, scaling and data drift; no matched runtime or reliability ranking |

Stochastic vs robust: common x minimizes c*x+sum pi_s Q_s(x); CVaR adds tail cost with auxiliary z/u_s, robust finite min-max imposes z>=Q_s(x) all s, robust weather near-optimal intersections instead constrain cost tolerance for each year. Different goals; scenario MC independent solves not common-x. Coincident heat/EV/H2/load/RES and outage inputs, chronology, probability vs duration, holdout scenarios, feasibility and slack all matter. For ERAA separate probabilistic LOLE/EENS and revenue-based EVA; PyPSA-GB capacity market and stochUC useful submodules, not compliant ERAA pipeline. Numerical robustness requires objective ledger, primal/dual residuals, integer gap, solver settings, AC replay, state continuity and sample convergence. Prior REV04 analytical oracles remain. No measured person-hours/runtimes, no license-cost comparison.
