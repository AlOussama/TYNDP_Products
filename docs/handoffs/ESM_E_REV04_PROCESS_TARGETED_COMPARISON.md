## 2. Comparative judgement by process

See machine-readable process fit matrix. The following prioritizes the next matched tests, not final production procurement or an official-study claim.

- Scenario Building: PLEXOS original-study reference if available; O24 reproducible public partial reference; GEMS-first and Eur-first are challengers for reusable broad sector modelling. GEMS is a language plus interpreters, not a prepopulated scenario database; Classic Antares alone is not the complete sector-coupled model. Reuse typed profiles, plant mapping, ETM and sector balances independently.
- IoSN: min c^T x + sum_s w_s Q_s(x), with Q_s = min operating cost over y_s satisfying network, availability and chronology. Common x, not independent weather-optimal x_s. Xpansion LP Benders is an attractive architectural competitor to extensive-form PyPSA/GemsPy, particularly scenario-parallel, provided LP recourse, scenario weights, dual and topology/candidate consistency. Explicit network equations from M2, not NTC simplification; modeler/export path must prove passive AC expansion routing and PST semantics (prior B converter gaps). PLEXOS LT as independent arm if study can be reproduced. If recourse has integer UC or endogenous topology nonlinearities, ordinary LP dual cuts are not an exact solution: define relaxation and bounds or qualify another method. Offshore and H2 multiports with shared activity. Separate IoSN successive real/conceptual candidate filtering from stochastic stages. Validate full annual chronology before aggregation.
- CBA: matched reference/project delta in objective, dispatch, losses, curtailment, costs and separately prescribed indicators. Project RES unlock and model input differences explain potential disagreements beyond formulation; not legitimate to calibrate solver to official ensemble total. O24 published benchmark gives strongest immediately reusable open workflow evidence; 2026 reference and weights remain blockers. PLEXOS and Antares official-study participation important; get case-specific reference data. Do not equate objective delta with all social welfare, all CBA indicators, discounted NPV or project ranking.
- H2/gas: PLEXOS actual process use and PyPSA-Eur sector implementation are starting points, GEMS transparent multi-carrier extensions. Pressure, compressor, linepack, hydraulic security and mutually exclusive retrofit need separately qualified equations or external solver. Country balances do not prove physical feasibility.
- ERAA EVA: compare actual legacy PLEXOS and French Antares revenue-based workflows. Revenue-based EVA makes repeated ED and resource-specific margins, risk, CM and other revenue streams, entry/exit/retirement decisions and convergence a first-class reusable process. No simple equivalence to social-cost LP/Xpansion or PyPSA system-cost expansion. Year-specific ACER approval and rollout plan; new method not retroactive. Capture net revenue as market income + qualified other revenues minus variable/avoidable/fixed costs as applicable; compare NPV and risk under identical convention, hurdle and candidate rules. Explicitly record stopping criterion, oscillation, repeated runs, economic signals, treatment of capacity-market payments and no double-counted risk. PLEXOS active 2026 implementation unknown; Antares national application is not ERAA certification. GEMS/PyPSA require outer EVA workflow unless new evidence establishes one.
- ERAA adequacy: all four can in principle implement ED kernels; Antares Classic and PLEXOS have documented native/use evidence for RAA, not only generic expressiveness. Hourly dispatch with weather/outage correlated availability, storage and hydro state, fuel/UC constraints, FB/NTC and scarcity sharing. Weather/outage IDs, stochastic generator and trace validation, sampling/weights/CI, pre/post sharing LOLE/EENS, exact threshold, deterministic tie-break. Reuse public ERAA inputs not assume access to complete commercial study. GEMS/PyPSA open research arm needs external MC/metrics/FB data/hydro orchestration (PyPSA FBMC release vs HEAD from C). France and ERAA use differing geographical extent, calendar, MC samples, hydro, FB and EVA; not a controlled benchmark. 
- Germany: separate Szenariorahmen regionalization/data approval, NEP market-to-grid dispatch and N-1/AC security, gas/H2 network physics, SES intertemporal whole-system optimization, PVSM risk and grid stress, reserve, stability, DSO and heat planning. No market engine alone covers all. PyPSA-Eur/DE provides reusable open interperiod and sector data; GEMS-first custom domain library and PLEXOS LT alternatives; Antares/Xpansion where scenario investment/adequacy central. eGon/eDisGo/FlexPlan and M2 are physical/regional complements, not a fifth primary model family. Keep grid and dynamic qualification distinct from economic optimization. Use national policy/approved scenario provenance; no inference of NEP or PVSM official tool use from generic capabilities.

## 4. Numerical reasoning and analytical references

The 12 new analytical oracles are in ESM_E_TARGETED_ANALYTICAL_ORACLES.csv; all synthetic. AO01 uses real published draft *weights* but synthetic demand/cost. AO03 exact probability enumeration, AO08 finite commitment statuses, others simple balances/linear bounds; not model or solver runs. They quantify why matched formulations matter. In particular:
- AO01 objective f(0)=11.9, f(1)=8.3, f(2)=8.0. Correct weighted common x=2; equal weights x=1; first weather x=0; independent weather choices expected 4.76 (perfect-information lower bound). Convex piecewise-linear kinks justify optimum on [0,2]. Do not report this as actual IoSN.
- AO03 independent two units: probabilities: exactly two healthy .81, one .18, zero .01. LOLE=.19, EENS=.18*.5+.01*1.5=.105. Perfectly correlated failures .1 -> EENS=.15, LOLE=.1. Deterministic derating loses both risk metrics. AO04 shows equal EENS/cost can yield different LOLE depending on dispatch ties; AO05 shows revenue streams/risk change entry. 
- AO02 storage chronology, AO06 FB domain, AO07 matched project benefit, AO08 integer vs LP, AO09 common multiport activity, AO10 redispatch, AO11 EV time/feeder, AO12 carrier retrofit are independent qualification tests, not performance or official-method validation.
- Test tolerances: propose energy/flow/constraint residual <= 1e-7 absolute + 1e-7 relative scaled by fixture magnitude; objective abs <= 1e-6 plus 1e-7 relative, exact declared binary/integer status, no relaxation; before larger runs set explicit MIP gap and independent feasibility/scale-aware thresholds; LOLE epsilon and sampling precision must follow process. These are pilot suggestions, not official tolerances.

## 5. Scalability evidence and execution design

Illustrative 8760-hour normalization: 540 MC years = 4730400 sample-hours, 1000 = 8760000, 4000 = 35040000; not actual runtime or number of solves, since calendars/rolling/pre-dispatch differ. Three weather cases in IoSN full annual extensive form = 26280 time-scenario indices per asset before any unit, port, constraint, year or network expansion; 100 zones = 2628000 zone-hour positions. Benders master common x and scenario subproblems may reduce simultaneous memory and enable parallel runs; iteration count, cuts, degeneracy, LP recourse, temporal cross-scenario constraints and overhead matter. GemsPy dense incidence vs sparse transformations is a test hypothesis; PyPSA native stochastic replication may increase matrix size. PLEXOS and Antares production scales not known from the application counts. No faster/slower claim.

Published OET Open-TYNDP 2024 result page: SB 897/2559 s with 29.3/29.6 GB for NT2030/2040, MSV 619/769 s and 30.7 GB, RH reference 915/1306 s and 5.8 GB. These are reported stages and not entire CBA campaign, not normalized across systems. S11 Enertile problem size is not peak memory or runtime. Hardware/solver/options and model differences preclude ranking. 
Pilot ladder: T1 AO01–12 + existing fixture subsets (all four matched independently); T2 authoring package and two interpreters, strict forward/reverse conversion only when relevant; T3 24/168h coupled model incl integer UC optional, fixed reference inputs; T4 8760h 1/3 weather for IoSN and CBA, increase zones/branches, add stochastic x vs LP Benders, shared network topology; T5 same ERAA traces 10/100/540 samples (not claim statistical convergence), then actual process sample count if qualified; T6 official study replication, full CBA indicators and national process case. Measure model build, export, solver time, wall-clock/data postprocessing, peak RAM per process/aggregate, problem rows/cols/nonzeros, gap, iterations, threads/hardware, solver release/license, objective residual, outcome and confidence interval. Report timeout/unsupported and failed runs, do not omit. No target execution this pass; prior fixtures retained; all T1–T6 pending.
