## 4. SB26 actual workflow boundary and comparison reference
The draft 2026 scenario workflow explicitly uses TSO ETM demand/PEMMDB capacity/PECD and demand-profile data, PLEXOS hourly electricity/H2 simulation, then the Supply Tool. Selected passenger EV and hybrid heat demand is recalculated rather than counted both as exogenous and endogenous. Synthetic fuel demand is specified; production/import allocation responds to operation. Fossil final demand is bypassed to the Supply Tool and corresponding fossil markets are not explicitly represented in this scenario model. The fixed outage pattern is not probabilistic adequacy. This is operation, not a general infrastructure expansion study. PLEXOS generic capability cannot be inferred absent because a scenario omits it, and cannot be inferred active just because a product offers it. For 2026 actual CBA/IoSN the run packages and configuration remain unverified. For 2024 Open-TYNDP NT, market model outputs and official CBA multi-model aggregate must remain distinct; do not identify the aggregate as a standalone PLEXOS oracle (P04/P20, C2).
