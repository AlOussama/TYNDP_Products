## 4. Evidence sufficiency and scope questions

**Q1. Is the scientific two-arm design falsifiable?** Yes at the *protocol* level: separate models, pinned inputs, independent oracles, native E04 inspection, six-state results, negative controls, D4 triggers and explicit stop conditions are described. This does **not** assert that any proposed tool can actually emit these exports.

**Q2. Are identical outputs sufficient to infer equivalent mathematics?** No. Native generated constraints, feasibility projections, objective transformations and adversarial cases are needed; output similarity is at most a bounded observation.

**Q3. Must different optimal portfolios be judged failures?** No, not absent a demonstrable violated obligation. Prove primal legality and admissible optimality, then distinguish invariant totals from non-invariant project/candidate KPIs. A certified range needs additional proof beyond sampled solutions.

**Q4. Does this define actual implementation/tool support?** No. PyPSA, Antares, Sienna and other frameworks have no assessed version/build, generated equations, model/solver evidence, feature matrix or real capability status in ML-C07.

**Q5. Does it cover all 48 provisional Requirements?** Yes at structural *planning* level by direct transcription of the ML-R00 48 primary R04 tests, their V/D/risk/owner blockers and C04 mappings. This does not certify full semantic/test strength for each SR and cannot add SR-012/-049.

**Q6. Can any pilot phase deliver G1 GO?** Not automatically. Methodology, original data, parameter and oracle authorisation, independent external review and a formal separate Gate decision remain necessary. **G1 BLOCKED**.
