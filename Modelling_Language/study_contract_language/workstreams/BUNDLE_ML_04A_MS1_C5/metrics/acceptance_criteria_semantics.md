# Acceptance criteria
Reuse C4 scoped native assertions or Comparison contracts, rather than a second threshold language. Equality and relative tolerances must use native mathspec conditions and compatible quantity types; zero-reference behavior is explicit (not an implied default). Conditional applicability is C4 guard/assumption semantics. Coverage is over finite declared support only. No PASS from unexecuted external review. General statistical inference, arbitrary decision rules, proof of whole-model behavior and independent oracle authenticity are outside the demonstrated fragment.
