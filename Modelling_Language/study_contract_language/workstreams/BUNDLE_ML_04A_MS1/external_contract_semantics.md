## Mathematical semantics
Let U be declared inputs, A:U→Bool3 assumptions, U_A={u | A(u)=true}, W_M(u) internal feasible behaviours, and π_M:W_M(u)→Y a typed external observation projection. B_M(u)={π_M(w): w∈W_M(u)}. Internal variables/equations are not contract identity. A complete verdict requires all relevant assumptions decidable and U_A nonempty. For every u∈U_A require B_M(u) nonempty; unknown/missing input, observation or support yields BLOCKED rather than PASS or false.

Safety: ∀u∈U_A ∀y∈B_M(u): S(u,y). Capability with externally declared admissible requests R(u): ∀u∈U_A ∀r∈R(u) ∃y∈B_M(u): C(u,r,y). Require nonempty request scope where applicability is asserted; explicitly inapplicable is not unknown. Actual service (when defined for every realised behaviour) uses ∀y∈B_M(u): Service(u,y); a capability for requests is not evidence that a chosen dispatch delivered service. For stochastic service, use ∃ admissible nonanticipative policy f for each declared predecision request and known information, ∀ compatible future scenarios ω, such that the resulting traces meet S and Service. The ordering of ∃policy and ∀ω is essential; ∀ω∃action may leak future information. Policy constant on information equivalence classes at each decision stage. Never infer information partitions from mere equal sample decisions.

For finite checks let O be complete observed support. An oracle checks predicate rows in O and returns PASS_WITNESS/FAIL_WITNESS/BLOCKED for that witness, not universal model conformance. Missing coordinates and undefined division are unknown/blocked. In strong Kleene Bool3: false AND unknown=false; true AND unknown=unknown; true OR unknown=true; false OR unknown=unknown; NOT unknown=unknown. Applicability unknown blocks an asserted obligation even if an evaluator would drop the row; guard false is inapplicable, and an empty applicability scope needs explicit authorization. The experiment conservatively rejects any unknown input before arithmetic; full Bool3 propagation and quantifier interpreter remain open.

## Distinguish proof levels
E1 canonical identity in a versioned normalization scope; E2 equality/equivalence of obligations over declared domains; E3 numerical agreement of aligned observed values; E4 external behaviour equivalence B_M1(u)=B_M2(u) for the agreed input scope (internal equation equality neither necessary nor sufficient); E5 separately established conformance to the same external contract. E5 does not require E4, and E3/E1 alone imply neither E4 nor E5. A model may be more restrictive than another while both meet the obligations provided capability remains satisfied. A complete proof or scientifically sufficient verification evidence must name assumptions, quantifier scope, tolerances, projection and independence. No promotion from sample success.

Native data assumptions may encode a pointwise predicate but do not represent B_M or its quantifiers. Native feasibility of a variable-based program is a different semantic object.
