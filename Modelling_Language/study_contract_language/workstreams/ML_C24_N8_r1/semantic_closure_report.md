# Semantic closure report — partial
NON-NORMATIVE / RESEARCH ONLY

## Implemented finite research slice
A textual S-expression and independently authored JSON B decode to a typed finite graph. Explicit domain and variable bindings, lexical scope, operator checks, observation axes, ordered quantifiers and typed guards are exercised. Canonical graph digest is separate from source-dependent audit identity. Original pinned C21 is invoked on a compatibility projection; it is not a validator for all R1 constructs.
## Proposed general semantics (not fully implemented)
For a typing context Gamma and scope S, a variable must belong to its declared domain; observations require valid component/port, carrier/unit and time/scenario support; expression types follow registry signatures; guard is Bool3, with UNKNOWN blocking a positive obligation result. Quantifier binder order is semantic. For a set T(A) of traces admitted by assumption A, safety requires every trace in T(A) to satisfy G. Capability requires for every admissible request r an admissible response e satisfying G, with information constraints and feasibility, not a single existential trace. Service requires an explicit service definition and temporal/conditional assumptions, and a guarantee over permitted interactions; no implication that safety, capability and service are interchangeable. Empty-domain and vacuity policy must be explicit. Information partitions and nonanticipativity restrict decisions measurable at each stage.
## Open implementation gaps
Full C20 candidate grammars and AST-to-CSNF mapping remain open. C21 compatibility projection substitutes guard representation; this is lossy and intentionally tested. The finite registry does not cover general ports, component interaction, scenarios/information partitions, temporal support, trace-based service obligations, or complete data/observable mappings. No complete rewrite-system soundness, semantic equivalence or comprehensive idempotence proof. The method AST is evaluated after C21, not a fully typed canonical CSNF subgraph. Source digest here is of UTF-8 text; raw byte identity and transport provenance need explicit policy. Unsupported or malformed structures must be rejected or blocked with structured diagnostics. No shrinking of agreed grammar scope.
