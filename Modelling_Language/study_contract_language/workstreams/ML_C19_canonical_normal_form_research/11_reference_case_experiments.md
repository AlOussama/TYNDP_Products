### RC01 Batterie
**Contract/Scope:** connected BUS_A (electricity, signed MW injection), Pcharge=3 MW, Pdischarge=4 MW, time delta in h, E0/Eterminal und eta_c/eta_d/standby loss *nur sofern methodisch festgelegt*. Guarantees Power envelope + korrektes Trace-Ledger + ggf. Service; Observables grid_flow, ledger_delta [MWh], availability.
**A/B:** A SOC(t)=E(t)/Emax, B energiebasierter interner Zustand in MWh; beide liefern dieselbe extern getypte Projektion. **CSNF:** ComponentRole(storage), interface(grid), guarded safety/ledger, output witness, variation internal state unrestricted. **Rewrite:** R06 kW/MW und R07 h/min nur mit expliziten Basen; E1 für bloße Port-Synonyme, E2 für identische outer rules; E4 interne Modelle nicht automatisch. **Positiv:** 2 MW Laden für 0,5 h => 1 MWh Netzaufnahme vor eta; beide bilanzierten Energiezeugen zulässig. **Negativ:** 2 MWh als Energieaufnahme oder Verletzung Terminal. **Freedom:** SOC, E, integral/convexification. **Mapping:** G Port-/Library-A/B; M je eigenes Spec; S Part/Port/Requirement; **Loss:** ohne Efficiency/Boundary kein E2. **Evidence:** DESIGN, native G/M/S NOT_EXECUTED.

### RC02 EV-Flotte
**Contract:** Fleet grid import MW, individual connection windows/arrival SOC and mobility-service deadlines; V2G disabled unless explicitly permitted. **A/B:** individual states/schedules versus aggregate envelope with individual service witness. **CSNF:** fleet role, optional vehicle referent links, scenario-dependent guard, capability quantifiers, signed port. **Rewrite:** unordered vehicle IDs sort only if identity preserved (R02), temporal windows never reordered. **E-status:** gleiche Oberflächenliste nicht E2, wenn Servicequantifier fehlt; E5 möglich bei garantierter Servicebasis. **Positiv:** B liefert prüfbare Einzelfahrzeugbedienung bei gleichem Contract. **Negativ:** aggregate energy okay, EV2 departure missed; export despite V2G=false. **Freedom:** explicit/aggregate dispatch. **Mapping:** G component-vs-fleet libraries; M different dims EV; S PartUsages, Requirements/Port; **Loss:** individuelle Deadline verschwindet bei bloßer Summierung. **Evidence:** CONCEPTUAL_ONLY.

### RC03 Elektrolyse
**Contract:** electricity consumption MW at BusE, H2 production kg/h at NodeH, heating value basis LHV/HHV versioned; thermodynamic/efficiency envelope and service availability if required. **A/B:** constant kWh/kg vs segment-dependent efficiency. **CSNF:** two carrier-specific ports + conversion relationship guarded by basis. **Rewrite:** unit conversions electrical W->MW, H2 kg/s->kg/h (R06), but mass-to-energy requires approved heating value. **E-status:** different efficiency curves may both satisfy bound; E5, not E4. **Positiv:** different internal nonlinearity with all outputs within obligation. **Negativ:** kg/h directly equals MW or HHV silently exchanged. **Freedom:** piecewise, continuous, heat recovery submodel. **Mapping:** G multiple fields/libraries, M expression per Spec, S Ports + constraint; **Loss:** energy-conversion basis. **Evidence:** CONCEPTUAL_ONLY.

### RC04 H2-Speicher
**Contract:** H2 mass in/out kg/h, mass inventory kg or clearly energy-tagged inventory MWh_LHV, leaks/losses, initial/terminal, power reconversion if present. **A/B:** explicit kg mass-state/compressor/FuelCell vs MWh_LHV energetic inventory with equivalent losses and port witness. **CSNF:** mass-flow ports, optional MW electric port, reference basis, external ledger. **Rewrite:** R06 with explicit kg-MWh conversion only if reference LHV constant and conversion context held. **E-status:** E2 obligation same, E3 bounded projection if tested, E4 not assumed. **Positiv:** 1 kg converted to declared LHV-based inventory. **Negativ:** identical numeric 1 interpreted as both kg and MWh; power without fuel trace. **Freedom:** internal thermodynamic states. **Mapping:** G Port/Binding, M dimensions/relations, S Port+Requirement; **Loss:** basis/temperature/pressure unspecified. **Evidence:** CONCEPTUAL_ONLY.

### RC05 Elektrisches Netz
**Contract:** fixed connected lossless linear-DC study assumption, bus injection and oriented branch MW, KCL **and** KVL, line susceptances/reactances, limits, slack/reference convention. **A/B:** angle + DC-Ohm/KCL versus correct cycle-flow or PTDF projection. **CSNF:** physical_branch vs bus + network property guarded by DC validity. **Rewrite:** R08 line orientation can invert with explicit sign adjustment, not change physical branch to market link. **E-status:** mathematical E4 under topology/reactance/slack assumptions needs a separate argument. **Positiv:** 3-bus lossless consistent Angle/Cycle/PTDF witness. **Negativ:** cycle circulation satisfying KCL but violating KVL; KCL-only contract distinct. **Freedom:** Angle, Cycle, PTDF internal. **Mapping:** G bus/branch + binding constraints, M indexed relations/constraints, S connection/type + requirement; **Loss:** KVL requirement disappears if only graph topology exported. **Evidence:** ML-C18 synthetic 27/27 historical, ML-C19 native NOT_EXECUTED.

### RC06 Ausbaukandidat
**Contract:** upgrade at Corridor A, commissioning time, capacity and cost/technical admissibility, indivisible modules if specifically mandated. **A/B:** continuous capacity choice versus discrete modules. **CSNF:** policy on discrete-vs-continuous is explicit obligation, not automatic language type. **Rewrite:** unit MW normalisation R06, not module granularity conversion. **E-status:** A and B may be E5 only if contract permits continuous approximation, otherwise DIFFERENT/FAIL; E4 generally false. **Positiv:** two differently implemented discrete methods output integer modules as required. **Negativ:** fractional 0.3 module treated buildable when integer modules REQUIRED. **Freedom:** Solver/algorithm within discreteness. **Mapping:** G different Libraries, M different Var domains, S Requirement; **Loss:** capex/builtability changed if discretization omitted. **Evidence:** DESIGN_ONLY.

### RC07 Investment/nonanticipativity
**Contract:** stage dates, lead time, scenario tree, information partitions by decision time; decisions before revelation identical across scenarios sharing same information. **A/B:** shared first-stage x vs scenario-indexed x with equality constraints for same information set. **CSNF:** decisionInformationGuard + required nonanticipativity property, not an index-list mandate. **Rewrite:** R02 may sort unordered scenario IDs, never stage/info-set ordering. **E-status:** E4 needs proof of equality of projected feasible investment sequences; E5 possible. **Positiv:** x(s1)=x(s2) before split. **Negativ:** stage-1 investment depends on unavailable weather signal, even though expected cost unchanged. **Freedom:** decision variable indexing. **Mapping:** G stage & scenario settings, M named dims/relations, S requirement & dependency; **Loss:** missing information-time semantics. **Evidence:** CONCEPTUAL_ONLY.

### RC08 Systemkosten/KPI
**Contract:** Total System Cost KPI with cost inclusions, price base/year, discount schedule, expected vs sample values, time/scenario weights, region and currency, multioptima selection. **A/B:** cost terms summed inside solver objective vs independent ledger postprocess. **CSNF:** ObservableDefinition(explicit cost basis and aggregation), VerificationRequest ledger reconciliation; objective not required identical. **Rewrite:** same EUR basis kEUR->MEUR (R12), not nominal->real without documented deflator. **E-status:** E2 KPI obligation if all basis attributes equal, E3 per sampled results after normalization. **Positiv:** identical auditable cost ledger after conversion. **Negativ:** OPEX without duration weight, CAPEX counted twice. **Freedom:** native objective decomposition. **Mapping:** G objective-contributions/views, M objective/expressions, S derived metric/constraint profile; **Loss:** objective vs KPI conflation. **Evidence:** DESIGN_ONLY.

### RC09 CBA
**Contract:** explicitly defined project vs counterfactual baseline, common exogenous scenarios, time horizon, price base, discount, indicators and decision rules. **A/B:** integrated project simulation-CBA vs decoupled energy simulation with postprocessing. **CSNF:** paired study frames, comparison relations, source authority, observable DeltaInd; **method authority N2E NOT_IDENTIFIED** and domain specifics OWNER_TBD. **Rewrite:** order independent indicator list, not baseline/project identities. **E-status:** only common defined KPI and basis yields E2; N2E release impossible without Owner decision. **Positiv:** two synthetic pipelines use same baseline and benefit definition. **Negativ:** demand differs between baseline/project without approved variation; discount bases differ. **Freedom:** computational workflow. **Mapping:** G study/Views only partial, M mathematical sub-spec, S requirement/analysis case; **Loss:** reference system and method authority. **Evidence:** SYNTHETIC_DESIGN, methodology BLOCKED.

### RC10 Multi-Model
**Contract:** shared external obligations for two independent model implementations, scenario/data snapshots, comparable Observatory schemas and allowed structure differences; distinguish conformance and variation/robustness. **A/B:** Model A angle/network+storage-state, Model B alternative PTDF+aggregate flexibility; both may be conformant without E4. **CSNF:** common ContractFrame + two ImplementationRefs + VariationPolicies + Observation projections + evidence lineage. **Rewrite:** stable sources/aliases/metric units allowed; R09 must keep separate model/source links. **E-status:** E5 permitted if each conforms; E3 only if relevant distributions/projections equal under S; E4 not necessary. **Positiv:** different outputs within defined allowed modelling envelopes, robustness metrics computed separately. **Negativ:** model B unreported load shedding allowance/extra exogenous scenario, result difference mislabelled robustness. **Freedom:** structural and algorithmic. **Mapping:** G different libraries, M separate specs, S external requirements and verification cases; **Loss:** model independence/provenance and apples-to-oranges comparison. **Evidence:** NO_EXECUTED_MULTI_MODEL.
