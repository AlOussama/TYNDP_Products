# Modelling freedom and evidence limits
RC01 energy state versus SoC needs explicit capacity and conversion witness; RC02 individual versus fleet aggregation requires feasible allocation by departure; RC03 constant versus variable efficiency only share obligations if both respect external conversion envelope; RC04 mass versus energy inventory requires heating-value basis; RC05 angle versus cycle formulation requires equivalent topology and KCL/KVL; RC06 integer count versus binary alternatives needs exact module/lead-time mapping; RC07 common policy versus scenario-indexed variables requires equal decisions on information classes. These are proof obligations, not proven equivalences. The finite RC fragments from C1 are retained as regression and do not materialize original A/B paths. No E2/E4/E5 promotion.
