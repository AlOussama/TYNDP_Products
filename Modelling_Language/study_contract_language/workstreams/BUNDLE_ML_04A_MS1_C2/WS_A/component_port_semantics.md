# Components, quantities, ports and time
Reuse R1 referents and port direction checks; every external port has component, direction (IN/OUT/BIDIRECTIONAL), carrier, quantity signature, axes, sign convention and time basis. A connection requires compatible opposite direction and quantity basis or an explicit source-bound conversion. Same dimensions alone do not equate heat, electricity or hydrogen. Power integrated with declared duration yields energy; mass rate integrated with duration yields mass. LHV/HHV conversion needs explicit heating value, source and valid range. An energy balance cannot mix injection-positive and withdrawal-positive terms without explicit orientation. C1 typed quantity algebra and conversion metadata are reused; no default factor. Money must bind currency, real/nominal, price year and source of conversion. 
 
Interval duration, chronology and initial/terminal conditions are explicit. A cyclic edge is not a zero-fill edge. Representative weights are not elapsed duration; chronology and weighting are independently declared. An information partition covers each scenario exactly once and refines over chronological stages; nonanticipativity compares decisions inside a common information class. Native relation keys, support, Sum, Join, Shift and WindowSum are reused in the admitted scope; sparse masks, partitioned shifts and arbitrary topology remain blocked unless separately admitted. RC05 KCL and KVL must both hold; RC06 discrete decisions include lead time/commissioning. R1's actual refinement and port checks are not yet bound to all native cases.
