# Physical quantities and conversion
Reuse C1 foundation quantity registry and checked carrier atoms, SI-like dimension algebra, explicit sign/direction/time basis, conversion metadata. MW and MWh differ by time, kg and kg/h by time, electric and heat energy by carrier, LHV and HHV by basis; these cannot be silently equated. Integration requires explicit duration and compatible quantity. A price has explicit EUR:real:YYYY or EUR:nominal:YYYY basis. Conversion factors are source- and version-bound positive METHOD_PARAMETER values; no default LHV/HHV ratio or inflation factor. Existing registry supports only documented units/conventions; arbitrary direction/sign composition, complete port topology and cross-currency conversions remain open. Native expressions are always traversed including unused branches. Actual tests: C1 foundation/closure, RC01/03/04/05 and C2-A04. See C1 physical_type_registry.json and closure.Checked.
