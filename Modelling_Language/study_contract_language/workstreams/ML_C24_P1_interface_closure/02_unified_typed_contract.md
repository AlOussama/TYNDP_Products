# Restricted typed contract r0.1

Nine-role C1–C9 frame/array envelope. Candidate B must explicitly declare all eight role collections, exact versions, unique IDs, qualified references, typed external ports (carrier, kind, unit, sign, direction, numeric anchor), typed observable projectors, scoped guarded assertions and immutable authority refs. Bool3, AND/OR/NOT, typed comparisons, forall/exists domain typing, power-time integration are the only accepted expression operators. Quantifier execution, full domain algebra, assumptions/guarantees over traces and universal physical invariants are NOT implemented. No unseen fields, unknown operators or implicit authority approvals accepted.

The `quantity.value=0` on a port is an explicit C21-compatibility anchor, **not** a measured port dispatch or prescribed internal decision variable. This is a known C21 type/value design issue; a fully faithful quantity-type / measured-value distinction remains required before soundness claims.
