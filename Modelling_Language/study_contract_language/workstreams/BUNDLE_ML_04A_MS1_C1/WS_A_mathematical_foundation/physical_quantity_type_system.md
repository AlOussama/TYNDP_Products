# Static quantity semantics

Reuse R1 Dimension algebra for independent physical and carrier/basis-tagged unit exponents. MW and kg/h differ physically; MW and MW_heat differ by carrier tags. No implicit scale conversion. Explicit typed ratios can encode LHV/HHV and cross-carrier factors; this is not conversion authority. Addition requires matching exponents and convention; multiplication/division combine tagged atoms. Direction, sign and time basis are explicit and conservatively propagated; general sign transformations, conversion provenance, weighting role semantics and arbitrary currency conversion are deferred. All admitted expression roots and external comparison guards are checked without data. Cases and macros fail admission. This is a narrow type algebra, not a general quantity system.
