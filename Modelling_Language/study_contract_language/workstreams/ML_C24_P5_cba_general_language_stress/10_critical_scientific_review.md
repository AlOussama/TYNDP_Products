# Independent critical research role – AI-only, no independent person

## Strongest supported findings
- C1–C9 concept covers CBA categories *at the level of role allocation*. It is not evidence of syntax completeness, full typed AST semantics or soundness.
- The independent P5 research oracle detects 30 designed mutations and preserves `OWNER_TBD`, unknown indicator values and unexecuted model evidence.
- Simple two-model numerical sample illustrates why method diversity and decision stability need separate evaluation; sign differences are not automatically source-invalid.
- CBA makes tool-independent study obligations testable as a **future** benchmark, without requiring all modelling engines to share mathematical formulation.

## Major limits / threats to validity
1. **Circular test-oracle risk:** P5 mutator and independent evaluator are co-authored. The benchmark is independent of the C20/C21 language but is not a person-independent or formally proven reference oracle; review must be external before promotion to B2.
2. **Synthetic bias:** the fixed 2×2×2 cost dataset is not a real TYNDP, electricity market, hydrogen or solver result. No full SEW/B1, B6 security, adaptation or distributional CBA quantified.
3. **Model diversity unproved:** labels `M-A` and `M-B` refer to synthetic records, not implemented independent generators. No CBA E5 conformance or robust sign verdict can be inferred.
4. **Normative ambiguity:** consultation pages are not final method-owner approvals; no current detailed final TYNDP cycle-specific method freeze was established. Regulations and method documents must be reviewed for authorised interpretation.
5. **Soundness gap:** the current P1/C21 architecture lacks a full CBA AST, scoped amount/time syntax, quantified semantics, complete source-preserving adapters, dual semantic/audit roundtrip and proofs.
6. **Coverage gap:** CBA primarily stresses study/counterfactual/output/governance semantics; physical port safety, storage terminal, EV service quantification, H2 conversion, KCL/KVL and some stochastic constraints require RC01–RC10 controls.
7. **Architecture comparison:** no native SysML/GEMS/mathspec experiment; no comparable A/B/C/H0 execution, measured complexity, true typechecker/normaliser cost or human usability.
8. **Oracle bias from methodological choices:** weights, cost proxy, discount horizon, project intervention and source status in the specimen are illustrative; no approval is being claimed.

## Specific design risk (critical)
Making all of `pairedDelta`, `weightedAggregate` and `discountedValue` primitive keywords would risk a study-specific core. First prove these cannot be assembled losslessly from general typed operations and controlled libraries. Conversely, representing them as free-text would lose semantics. Prefer typed profiles / library composition, not strings or premature C10+ roles.

## Disqualifying gates
No B1_MET until the real pinned Python C21 validator accepts complete typed examples and rejects negative original RC01 and CBA faults; no B2_MET based on P5 synthetic microcheck counts; no B3 architecture decision before comparable evidence; no G1 normative approval without owner.
